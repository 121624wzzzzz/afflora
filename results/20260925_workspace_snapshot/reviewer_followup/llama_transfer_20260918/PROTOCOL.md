# Llama transfer of the two previously positive downstream tasks

Frozen before any Llama task result. Models: official pretrained Meta Llama-3.2-3B and Llama-3.1-8B. Official pinned git/LFS identities independently checked for every inference file. This is a model-family transfer study, not a pure size comparison: versions, tying and pretraining differ. CLUENER is Chinese and language coverage may affect transfer; retain every outcome.

Exact examples, splits, prompt text and scorers copied and individually hashed against the sealed Qwen study model_architecture_14h_20260917. No fitted checkpoint/results reused. CLUENER: 2048/200/1343 train/dev/test; WikiSQL: 2048/256/1024. Public dataset pretraining exposure cannot be excluded. No truncation, output repair or result-dependent eligibility. Native Llama BOS 128000 added exactly once to the raw prompt and EOS 128001 exactly once to the target; no chat template. Prompt/target boundary remains explicitly separately tokenized as in the Qwen study. Pad uses EOS with explicit masks. Target lengths checked before fitting against unchanged generation caps 512 NER, 256 SQL.

Four arms per model/task: Base (one deterministic evaluation), H (ordinary internal LoRA), budget H, H+E+U. Three paired predetermined seeds 6100/6101/6102 for CLUENER and 7100/7101/7102 for WikiSQL. Total 36 fits and 4 Base evaluations; 8 two-step technical smoke jobs separately retained. Smokes have no metric pass threshold and never enter formal results.

Internal H: rank 8, alpha16, dropout .05 at q/k/v/o/up/down/gate. Boundary E/U: separate rank16 alpha128 scale8 dropout0 residual maps; E has bias, U no bias. Boundary total 65*d. Frozen original weights. Budget H spends at least 65*d on deterministic evenly spaced q/k rank increments, minimizing excess with the same allocation rule as Qwen, preserving common rank8 initialization and scale2. Llama-3.1-8B: exactly 21,237,760 trainable parameters for both budget H and H+E+U. Llama-3.2-3B: budget H 12,357,632 versus H+E+U 12,356,608 (+1024; 0.00829% of treatment trainable count). No inert padding parameters; disclose approximate match. No tuning ranks, placements or LR after results.

Identical training recipe: 2048 examples, one epoch, effective batch32, 64 steps, LR2e-4, AdamW(.9,.999), eps1e-8, weight_decay0, clip1, 2 warmup steps then cosine. BF16 frozen base, FP32 adapters/optimizer moments; BF16 autocast training; answer-plus-EOS token-mean loss across effective batches. NER microbatch2, SQL microbatch4. Final checkpoint only. FP32 greedy evaluation, TF32 disabled, SDPA; NER dev8/test32, SQL dev/test16. Native EOS stopping. All arms share task/model evaluation batches.

Before fit: exact trainable whitelist/count, actual tied pointer, zero-residual equivalence, independently shifted/masked HF loss equivalence. During fit: frozen gradient absence and finite gradients. After fit: frozen-base digests, every requested group changed, save/zero/reload exact loss, adapter hashes, sample-order audit. All generated records independently decoded and rescored; every valid SQL prediction checked against the official execution engine. Pair audit checks common hidden initialization, shared sample order, and both counts. Scorers audited against inherited official golds/negative cases.

Primary metrics: CLUENER span micro-F1 and WikiSQL official execution accuracy. Secondary schema/format and logical-form metrics reported separately. For each of four model/task conditions, paired H+E+U minus H and minus budget H: fixed family of 8 planned comparisons, even if any block is incomplete. Report all three raw differences, mean, SD, unadjusted descriptive 95% seed-t CI and Bonferroni-8 95% CI, df2. Only complete common-seed four-arm blocks enter inferential summaries. This is a new exploratory extension; prior Qwen comparisons are not pooled into a new confirmatory claim. Three positive signs do not alone establish statistical significance.
